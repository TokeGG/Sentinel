# Price tracking checklist

1. Take the product name (and model number if given) and the last known price from the request or the user's notes.
2. Check 2-4 shops. For each: price, currency, stock status, shipping cost, whether the price needs a membership or coupon.
3. Make sure it is the same model/variant (size, colour, storage) before comparing.
4. Compare to the last known price: lower, same or higher, and by how much.
5. Say "good time to buy" only if the lowest price is at or below the last known low; otherwise "wait".
6. Never add to cart, sign in or buy. Report shop, price and link for each check.
