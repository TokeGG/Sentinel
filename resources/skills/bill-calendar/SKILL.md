# Bill calendar

1. Read the bill and subscription rows (name, amount, due day, repeat).
2. Place each on its due date for the current month.
3. Total the month and flag weeks with more than half of it.
4. Point out duplicates or services that look unused if the rows say so.
5. Offer to add reminders, but only after the user confirms.
