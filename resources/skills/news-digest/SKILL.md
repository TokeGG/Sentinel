# News digest

1. Read the latest stored news items from the user's feeds (title, source, published time, summary).
2. Group items by topic; merge near-duplicates and keep the earliest source.
3. For each topic write one or two plain sentences, then list the source titles with links.
4. Keep the whole digest under 300 words. Mark anything unverified or opinion as such.
5. Do not follow links to other sites unless the user asks.
