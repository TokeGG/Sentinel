# Flashcards from notes

1. Read the notes and pick the facts worth remembering: definitions, dates, formulas, cause and effect, lists.
2. One fact per card. The question must make sense on its own (name the topic in it).
3. Keep answers short (one sentence or a list of at most four items).
4. Add cloze cards ("The capital of X is ___") for single key terms.
5. Skip anything the notes mark as uncertain; do not add facts the notes do not contain.
6. Output as lines "Q: ... | A: ..." so they can be imported.
