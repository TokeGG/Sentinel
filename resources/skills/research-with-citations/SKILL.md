# Research with citations

1. Restate the question and what a good answer contains. Plan 2-3 searches with different wording.
2. Search; open the most relevant results from different sites (prefer official sources, reference works and reputable news over forums).
3. From each page note the claim, the exact page link and the date it was published or updated.
4. Read at least 3 sources. When sources disagree, say so and show both sides.
5. Answer in short paragraphs; after each claim put the source number [1], [2]... and list the sources (title + link) at the end.
6. Only cite pages you actually visited in this run. Say plainly what you could not find.
