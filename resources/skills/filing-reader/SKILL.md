# Filing reader

1. Identify the company, period and filing type from the row.
2. Extract revenue, net income, and any guidance figures exactly as written.
3. Compare with the previous period only if both numbers are present.
4. Flag one-off items the text names.
5. Keep to the document; do not predict the share price.
