# Budget review

1. Group spending into categories (housing, food, transport, subscriptions, fun, other) unless the user already has categories.
2. Total each category and compare with the budget; show over/under in amount and percent.
3. List recurring subscriptions and their monthly cost; flag duplicates and ones not used recently (ask the user).
4. Suggest at most three concrete savings with an estimated monthly amount each.
5. Keep the tone neutral; do not lecture.
6. Never move money, sign in to a bank or share the numbers anywhere.
